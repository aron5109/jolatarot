# Chart

Stack: React 19 + TypeScript, Vite, plain CSS
Platforms: web (phone first, also desktop)
Visits: on

## Top tasks
1. Shuffle the deck, pick one or three cards and read the reading (single page)
2. Copy the reading to share it

## Users and context
Staff, for fun during Advent, mostly on a phone between other things. Icelandic only.

## Brand
- Color: spruce green (#0a231c, #123f32) and gold (#e2b865, #f3d58f) on cream cards
- Type: EB Garamond true small caps on the cards and position names, Playfair Display for headings, Manrope for body text
- Keep: hand-drawn SVG card art, the night sky with aurora, snow and spruce forest

## Accepted
- glow-shadow on `.star`: starlight is the backdrop, not a UI glow. Stops with reduced motion. 2026-11-16
- Aurora blur and radial background: it is the Icelandic winter sky, the product's setting. 2026-11-16
- Sparkles inside the card illustrations: they are stars in the artwork, not AI icons. 2026-11-16
